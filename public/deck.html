02 / 08
BOUND · HOW IT WORKS
A CERTIFICATE ANYONE CAN CHECK

Three parties,
one object

An operator publishes a certificate naming a bound — the maximum loss it will cover — and funds a reserve against it. An auditor stakes their own slashable capital attesting the bound is honest. A challenger who proves fraud is paid from the liar's money.

Three of the four fraud proofs are arithmetic on on-chain state. No referee: the contract reads the vault and does the subtraction itself.
